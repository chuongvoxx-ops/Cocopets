## Dungeon 11 The Choir Before Creation

**Recommended level:** 95\. **Affinity:** Wind and Dark\. **Mythos connection:** an original fragment of a primordial dream associated in this game with Azathoth, not Azathoth itself\. **Seal Sigil:** Rhythm\.

### Environment and route

A mountain’s interior has become a vast hollow musical instrument\. Use deep maroon `#341C2F`, bone `#D9C9A5`, tarnished gold `#A58A4E` and soft coral `#DA8F86`\. The ground consists of broad circular plates linked by short bridges\. Giant hollow pipes curve above them and disappear into darkness\. Give the pipes three different silhouettes: straight columns, low arches and large spirals\. Their openings pulse with soft light, creating the sense that the entire cavern is breathing\.

The entry corridor slopes into a circular chamber framed by two bone horns\. The route continues through the Silent Score rehearsal floor, the drum chamber for boss one, the Breathworks pipe gallery, a pipe bridge for boss two, the Held Note chamber of suspended bells and the final resonating bowl\. There is no audience, orchestra or humanoid choir; the architecture and alien organisms produce the sound\.

The final arena is a 36\-metre circular floor divided into four broad quadrants with different embossed symbols\. Outside its edge stand four enormous curved horns pointing inward\. A dark central heart floats at chest height\. The heartbeat makes the floor’s rings brighten; it does not shake the camera by default\. Provide a reduced\-motion setting for decorative pulsing\.

### Three distinct dungeon challenges

The three music\-themed tasks are reading and playing a visible score, routing air through a pipe network and holding a charged note while fighting\. Audio always has a complete visual equivalent\.

### Monster roster

- **Bell mites:** round maroon shells with three gold legs and a small hanging bone clapper\. Their shells visibly widen before a short\-range pulse\.
- **Pipe eels:** thin ivory tubes bent into S\-shapes, with a dark trumpet\-like mouth\. They fire three projectiles on a visible beat shown by three mouth lights\.
- **Hush moths:** black oval bodies with two broad cape\-like wings and a coral throat\. They silence a pad temporarily until interrupted; the effect is local and never disables the player’s entire ability kit\.

### Dungeon section 1 The Silent Score

**Route and appearance:** enter between two giant bone horns and descend onto a round rehearsal floor\. Four broad drum pads surround a suspended stone tablet\. Each pad bears a distinct embossed symbol matching one on the tablet\. Deep maroon pipes frame the room without crossing its walkable centre\.

**Unique challenge — perform the visible sequence:** stand on or command the pet to the pads in the displayed three\-symbol order\. The current step stays highlighted\. A second short phrase introduces a two\-second bright activation interval with a visible countdown border\. Space the pads so the actual running speed permits the required movement comfortably\.

**Monsters:** three bell mites defend the tablet\. Two pipe eels and a hush moth guard the second phrase\. The moth temporarily silences one pad until interrupted, clearly identifying the priority enemy\.

**Completion and recovery:** complete both phrases and clear their guards\. Wrong notes reset only the current phrase\. The final correct note opens the horn\-shaped gate into Tammor’s drum chamber\.

### Boss 1 Tammor the Living Drum

**Appearance:** a low six\-legged beast with a circular body four metres across\. Its back is a taut pale membrane stretched inside a maroon shell\. Three gold spikes protrude around the rim\. Its head is a small eyeless wedge beneath the membrane, with two blunt feelers\. It drums against the floor using its front pair of legs, and the back membrane visibly depresses before a shockwave\.

**Arena:** a 28\-metre drum chamber with three concentric floor rings, each clearly numbered outward from the centre\.

**Mechanics:** Threefold Beat lights the rings in a displayed order, then strikes them in that order\. Move into a ring after it resolves\. Hold the Silence inserts one empty beat, shown by a closed\-mouth icon, during which the boss takes increased damage\. At 50%, one beat becomes a frontal cone rather than a full ring, shown on the same preview\. There must always be a reachable adjacent safe ring\.

### Dungeon section 2 The Breathworks

**Route and appearance:** Tammor’s chamber opens into a broad pipe maintenance gallery\. A large bellows sends visible coral pulses into three thick bone conduits\. Each conduit ends in a horn with a pressure gauge and a different symbol\. Sliding shutters can route air toward the horns or into obvious open drains\.

**Unique challenge — make the three horns breathe together:** clear the blocked central conduit, close the waste drain and position two shutters so air reaches all three horn receivers\. A moving light packet shows every connection\. Each correct horn stores its pressure; once all three gauges fill, a combined note unlocks the exit\. This is a flow\-routing puzzle with no musical memory or beat\-input requirement\.

**Monsters:** two pipe eels and three mites occupy the main gallery\. A hush moth nests inside the central blockage and must be removed before that conduit can carry air\. Two more mites approach the shutter controls during the final fill\.

**Completion and recovery:** fill the three gauges and defeat the remaining enemies\. Incorrect shutter states harmlessly vent air and can be changed freely\. The pressurised horns unfold a short bridge onto Veyra’s platform\.

### Boss 2 Veyra the Throat of Night

**Appearance:** a bat\-like predator with a narrow bone chest, two enormous maroon wings and a long coral throat sac\. Its head is a smooth black wedge with no eyes and two tall antenna\-like ears\. The wings have only three major finger supports each, giving them a clear fan shape\. Its throat inflates into a glowing sphere before a sonic attack\. It usually clings upside down to a low arch, bringing its head within melee reach, then drops to the floor between phrases\.

**Arena:** a 32\-by\-28\-metre pipe platform with three horn mouths along the far edge and three shelter wedges near the centre\.

**Mechanics:** Antiphon lights horn mouths in an order; each fires a broad lane on its beat\. Take cover behind the appropriate wedge or move into the last discharged lane\. Stolen Note creates one hush moth that changes an upcoming horn’s symbol\. The changed preview visibly updates at least four seconds before it resolves\. At 50%, the boss drops into the arena and uses a wing cone between horn attacks\. Its landing creates a generous damage opportunity\.

### Dungeon section 3 The Held Note

**Route and appearance:** Veyra’s platform leads into a chamber of three suspended bells above a wide circular charge pad\. Two side alcoves provide space for fighting\. A large ring around the pad fills with coral light, while the final resonating bowl is visible through a closed gold lattice\.

**Unique challenge — sustain charge under pressure:** accumulate thirty total seconds of pad activation\. The pet can Hold on it while the handler fights, or the handler can stand there and direct the pet outward\. A broad cone occasionally targets the holder, requiring a temporary leave or a controlled swap\. Leaving the pad pauses charge rather than erasing it\.

**Monsters:** the first wave has three mites; the second two eels and a mite; the third a hush moth with two mites\. Only one wave is active at a time\. The moth must be interrupted to resume charging\.

**Completion and recovery:** fill the ring and defeat all three waves\. Charge remains through temporary interruptions, and a downed pet can be recalled or revived using the shared recovery rules\. The bells lift the gold lattice, opening the passage into the Heart That Hears\.

### Boss 3 The Heart That Hears

**Appearance:** a floating, roughly spherical heart made from three thick dark\-maroon lobes, six metres across\. Four bone tubes emerge from its top and curve outward like a crown of horns\. A gold ring is embedded around its middle, broken in two places\. It has no face\. During each beat the gaps in the ring reveal a soft coral interior\. Four short tendrils connect it to the floor, keeping its vulnerable lower surface within reach\. Avoid realistic veins or gore; it should resemble an alien musical seed\.

**Mechanics:** above 70%, Pulse Measure previews three quadrant attacks on the embedded ring, then plays them in order\. From 70–35%, Dissonance adds an off\-beat echo marked by a smaller second outline\. The player must wait for the echo before entering a freshly struck quadrant\. Two hush moths try to obscure a resonator, but the main preview always remains legible\. Below 35%, The Unfinished Chord displays a four\-step sequence ending in a sustained whole\-arena pulse\. Charge the indicated drum pad during the first three steps to create shelter for the fourth\. The pet can hold the pad while the handler moves around it\. After the sheltered pulse, the ring breaks open for a long vulnerability window\. This is anticipation and route planning, never a demand for frame\-perfect musical timing\.

**Victory:** every pipe falls silent for two seconds, then one gentle chord plays\. The heart becomes a motionless dark seed around the Rhythm Sigil\. Reward a small bell accessory with an option to mute its cosmetic sound\.
