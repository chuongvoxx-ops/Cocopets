# Rift 9 — The Basilica of the Veiled Eye: build brief

The user's design is `docs/rift9-spec.md` (follow it faithfully: names, colours, monsters, the three challenges, the three
bosses and their mechanics). The rift's plan entry already exists in `src/js/21b-rift-ui.js` (`id: 'basilica'`, n 9,
lvl 85, sigil Veil, gate anima `libra` "The Suspended Garden" — guide a pearl by balancing a board) and its world-map
node is drawn (`23b-world-map.js`). Everything below is what has to be created. Read `docs/NOTES.md` sections
"v144 — RIFT 10" and "v140 — Rift 8" first: they list exactly what a finished rift consists of.

## Files (alphabetical load order matters: stages before core, core before gate and bosses)
- `src/js/20zt-rift-09-stages.js` — `const BAS_STAGES = { PILGRIMAGE, CONTAINMENT, PROCESSION }` (the three challenges; model: `20zn-rift-10-stages.js` → COL_STAGES, and `20zh-rift-08-stages.js`).
- `src/js/20zt2-rift-09-basilica.js` — the core (model: `20zo-rift-10-colours.js`): `const BAS = {}` palette + shared helpers, three lands/biomes with tiles, walls, props and decals, the brood (3 monsters), the three evil-Cocopet minis, `defRift({...})` with `riftSections` (I fight + PILGRIMAGE + Eshra | II CONTAINMENT + fight + Thessarym | III fight + PROCESSION + Gharaal), `ease: 0.55, monEase: 0.5` like rifts 7–10, the SEALING banner (Veil Sigil), `everyStage` for the red ceiling-slit light stripes (desaturated, unoutlined, purely decorative).
- `src/js/20zu-rift-gate-libra.js` — the Libra gate (model: `20zp-rift-gate-sagittarius.js`, `20zj-rift-gate-pisces.js`): "The Suspended Garden": a hanging stone board the player tilts by standing on it (the pet's position on the board is the tilt); a pearl rolls on it toward three blossom cups in turn, past holes; three pearls delivered wins. 3–5 minutes.
- `src/js/20zv-rift-fiend-basilica-1.js` Eshra the Many-Lidded, `20zw-rift-fiend-basilica-2.js` Thessarym the Veil Serpent, `20zx-rift-fiend-basilica-3.js` Gharaal the Buried Witness (model: `20zq/20zr/20zs-rift-fiend-colours-*.js`, and `20z1-rift-fiend-sepulchre-1.js` Threnn for the art bar). Boss ids: `eshra`, `thessarym`, `gharaal`. Each registers `RIFT_FIENDS.<id>` and its painter/AI the way the colours bosses do.
- Tests: `tests/rift-bas-stages.js`, `tests/rift-bas-gate.js`, `tests/_boss-anim-basilica-{eshra,thessarym,gharaal}.js` (models: `rift-col-stages.js`, `rift-col-gate.js`, `_boss-anim-colours-*.js`); add them to `runbat.sh`.

## Shared mechanics (live in the core as `BAS.*`, used by the stages and by bosses 2 and 3)
- **Screens on rails** `BAS.screen(X, H, { x, y, a, stops: [[x,y],[x,y]], len, i0 })`: a rigid pale stone sheet (len tiles wide, 1.6 tall) that slides between fixed stops along a short black rail; the player (or the pet by Hold) moves it by standing at its handle and tapping/holding the use button, one second per stop; `BAS.shadowOf(screen, source)` returns the polygon of floor its shadow covers from a light/eye source; `BAS.inShadow(x, y, source)` whether a point is covered by any screen from that source. Draw the shadow as a double-bordered darker region with a closed-eye icon.
- **Eye pylons / gaze cones** `BAS.pylon(X, H, { x, y, angles: [a0,a1,a2], period, len, spread })`: a tall red-eye pylon that turns among fixed angles with a visible preview (a dim dashed cone 1 s before it turns), casting a watched cone; a point is "seen" when inside the cone and not in a screen's shadow. **Calcification**: `BAS.calcify` tracks the pet's stages 0–3 while seen (one stage per 0.8 s), shown as grey stone creeping up a HUD meter and over the pet's feet; leaving the cone clears them over 1.5 s; stage 3 roots the pet 1.2 s and deals 20% of max HP, then releases and resets. Gaze is never a camera-facing rule: it's geometry only.
- **Witness scarab cone**: the monster's own slowly rotating narrow cone, the same seen/shadow test, feeding Calcification.
- Monsters: calcite leech (still, spits slow petrification droplets that add Calcification stacks on hit), witness scarab (eye plate, rotating cone, slow turn), broken sentinel (headless four-legged stone beast; a red orb floats where the head was; it charges only after the orb's lock line — a thin red line to the pet — has been unbroken for 1 s). Minis: three RiftEvil evil Cocopets (`veilpet`, `witnesspet`, `basaltpet`) like `bleachpet` etc. in 20zo.

## The three challenges (one each, stations as `H.obj`, exits via `H.setExit`)
1. THE BLIND PILGRIMAGE (land 1, outer cloister): three pylons, pale screens on short rails; slide each screen to its stop that blocks the next pylon's cone, cross in its shadow; guards per spec; the last shadow holds the latch to Eshra's ossuary.
2. THE WITNESS CONTAINMENT (land 2, courtyard): three basins (high rim, two gaps, closed-eye centre); three scarabs released one at a time from pens with their escorts; kill each scarab inside a basin (its death position) to close one exit lock; a scarab killed outside drops a carriable eye stone to carry into an empty basin (recoverable). Three locks → Thessarym's reliquary.
3. THE PROCESSION OF STONE (land 3, the avenue): one broad screen on a central rail moving between three stops; three pylons watch successive sections; at stops 1 and 2 the pet holds the handle (use button) while the player opens a side-alcove latch (the screen waits while a latch is closed); a failed gaze (stage 3) returns the pet to the latest stop with damage; the scarab at the final arch; the screen parks as part of the wall → Gharaal.

## Bosses (each a fiend file; art to the Threnn standard in NOTES "BOSS ART STANDARD": many parts in depth order, 3-tone ramps per material, tube shading, never still, wind-up poses, artPoses for the anim test)
- Eshra the Many-Lidded (stage 2): four-metre calcite scorpion, low rectangular body, six short jointed legs, tail ending in a big red eye framed by three stone lids that surveys independently; shield claws with black inner faces. Arena: ossuary with three permanent square pillars (triangle). Witness Ray (red tether to the pet, 3 s, broken by a pillar between), Claw Crush (frontal wedge), at 50% the eye marks a pillar to crack after the next shot (one extra cycle before it goes).
- Thessarym the Veil Serpent (stage 5): eight-metre snake of seven pale stone segments with dark gaps, one closed eye per segment, broad triangular head with hooked side fins and no jaw, a red vertical eye opening only when attacking; coils in a low ring. Arena: circular reliquary, two screens on N–S rails, central safe lane. Passing Judgement (segment eyes open in numbered order, narrow lanes fire in sequence), Unveiling (broad frontal beam locks then freezes direction: pull a screen into it), at 50% a tail sweep during the beam charge.
- Gharaal the Buried Witness (stage 9, final): a living basalt altar, ten-metre faceted mound on eight low thick limbs, five calcite horns like ruined columns, one enormous red eye behind four wedge lids, two small paddle arms that drag screens; red-glowing cracks before the eye opens; moves by heaving. Arena: the 38 m square sanctuary, the sealed wall-eye on the north wall, four screens on short rails. Above 70% Slabfall (three marked rectangles, one limb exposed for a counter); 70–35% The Unveiling (major gaze: get pet and screen shadow aligned before the cast ends; then it drags that screen one stop); below 35% Witnesses Within (two back eyes open 2 s apart, cones leave an intersection of safe shadows; slide a screen, stand in the shared shadow, destroy a calcite eye node to force the main eye shut). Victory: the wall eye closes, calcified plants regain colour, banners crumble; the Veil Sigil; the closed-eye talisman as the seal reward (an accessory: see how rift 10 grants the ORBITAL RING, ACC_SPECIAL).

## Rules
- Palette from the spec: volcanic black #242428, ochre #A18E65, calcite #D8D1BB, muted red #BF675D (hostile cones/warnings keep the game's red-orange fills; the decorative red light stripes are desaturated and unoutlined so they never read as lanes).
- Statues are abstract beasts curled round eye stones (no crowds of humanoid figures). Stone banners hang rigid.
- Keep existing camera, controls, pet AI, hold/recall; nothing is unavoidable because of where the camera faces.
- Every file: build (`python3 src/build.py --test-only test-bas.html`), `node --check` the extracted script if the page won't load, run your tests with `HTML=test-bas.html`, look at screenshots (SHOTS=1) and iterate. Do not commit.
