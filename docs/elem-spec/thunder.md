# LIGHTNING (element key `thunder` everywhere in code; keys in ELEM_MOVES.thunder, in this order)
Vocabulary: branching arcs, electrical lattices, charged nodes, rapid connections, pulsing fields and sequential discharges. Colours: electric yellow (#FFD447), white-hot cores (#FFFFFF), blue-white arcs (#CFEFFF, #9FD8FF), violet-blue plasma shadows (#4A3FA8) for the dark side of plasma shapes. Arcs are jagged polylines with an additive glow and a thin white core, regrowing every few frames (jitter), never static. Keep it distinct from Light (no smooth rays or stained glass). Use H.X.zap (arc between points), H.X.bolt (sky bolt), stun via dmgO({ stun }).

## STRONG
forkedfang — LI-S01 Forked Fang: Two short electrical lances fire together, twisting around a shared path. They separate just before impact to strike nearby targets or converge on one enemy. Thin branching arcs connect the lances briefly, then retract into bright pinpoints.
stormbead — LI-S02 Stormbead Chain: Several charged beads appear around the target and discharge between nearby enemies in sequence. Each bead swells slightly before firing, making the chain readable. The final bead breaks into a spray of short-lived blue-white sparks.
voltscythe — LI-S03 Volt Scythe: A curved electrical blade forms along the pet's sweeping motion. Its outline is stable while smaller arcs crawl across its surface. The blade travels a short distance after the swing, delivering a broad cone strike.
thunderpin — LI-S04 Thunderpin: A slim charged spike lands at the selected point. Branching arcs crawl down its length before it sends three short pulses into a small surrounding area. The spike then splits into vertical light fragments and vanishes.
railflash — LI-S05 Railflash: Two parallel electrical lines appear briefly along the aimed corridor. A concentrated bolt races between them, piercing enemies with a sharp impact. Residual sparks chase the bolt's route a moment later and quickly fade.
staticblossom — LI-S06 Static Blossom: Small electrical branches sprout outward from a central charged point on the ground. Their tips divide and connect, forming a brief expanding network. A pulse travels through the network once, dealing area damage before the branches extinguish.
arcboomerang — LI-S07 Arc Boomerang: A V-shaped plasma arc launches forward while rotating slowly. It curves back toward the pet after reaching its limit, striking on both passes. Loose sparks trace the turn, helping players judge the returning damage path.
sparkstep — LI-S08 Sparkstep Echo: The pet releases a short electrical strike in the aimed direction while a charged outline lingers at its starting point. The outline detonates after a brief delay. Movement: use the game's dash — set `H.me.dash = { vx, vy, t, max: t, hit: new Set(), dmg, land: 0, el: C.el }` for a short 2-tile dash (see 's_rush' in 20b-game-dungeon.js useStrong) so the existing dash system moves the pet.
capacitor — LI-S09 Capacitor Crush: Two floating charged plates appear on opposite sides of the target. Arcs jump between them as they draw closer, dealing repeated small hits. They clap together in a concentrated discharge, then break into dim geometric sparks.
stormthread — LI-S10 Stormthread Volley: Thin electrical threads connect the pet to several charged needles hovering ahead. The needles fire in rapid succession, their threads snapping into brief jagged trails. The final projectile leaves a branching star-shaped impact on its target.

## ULTIMATES
thunderking — LI-U01 Palace of the Thunder King: Tall charged pylons rise around the selected zone, linked by arching electrical cables made of light. Pulses circulate through the structure before jumping downward into marked areas. The pylons tilt their upper segments inward and discharge together, creating a central lightning column surrounded by spiralling fragments of electric geometry.
ninedragon — LI-U02 Nine-Dragon Circuit: Nine slender lightning dragons form from branching arcs around the pet. They race along distinct curved paths through the target area, leaving short-lived conductive trails. The trails connect into a glowing circuit, which discharges in sequence before the dragons merge into one final descending bolt.
capacitorsky — LI-U03 Celestial Capacitor: Two enormous segmented rings rotate above the battlefield, building charge between their surfaces. Smaller arcs repeatedly leap across the gap as targeted strikes fall below. The rings align, compress, and release a broad downward discharge. Their segments drift apart afterward, shedding fading sparks along their edges.
thunderloom — LI-U04 Thunderloom: Charged nodes form at different heights around the selected area. Electrical threads weave between them into a three-dimensional lattice. Pulses race through different rows, each triggering clearly timed damage beneath it. The entire lattice contracts into a central knot and bursts outward as a final branching shockwave.
stormbird — LI-U05 Stormbird Descent: A huge bird made of sharp plasma ribbons unfolds above the pet, its wing edges constantly branching into smaller arcs. It dives through the aimed corridor, leaving successive ground strikes beneath its path. At the endpoint, it spreads its wings and discharges a broad fan of lightning before dissolving into sparks.
magnetar — LI-U06 Crown of the Magnetar: A brilliant core appears above the selected point, surrounded by rotating metallic-looking effect shards and electrical rings. The shards orbit faster as arcs leap between them. They fire outward in controlled waves of charged projectiles, then the exposed core releases one final expanding electrical pulse.
thousandbells — LI-U07 Thousand-Bell Thunder: Small bell-shaped electrical constructs appear above enemies in staggered rows. Each swings once and releases a forked bolt downward, producing a rhythmic barrage. Their remaining outlines connect into a huge suspended bell, which delivers a final circular discharge and breaks into a shower of tiny blue sparks.
heavenrail — LI-U08 Rail of the Heavens: Parallel streams of charged particles build a long corridor in the aimed direction. Floating accelerator rings assemble along it and light up sequentially. A dense plasma lance fires through the rings, followed by delayed branching discharges along the route. The rings collapse one after another into fading electrical spirals.
lightningtree — LI-U09 Lightning Tree Ascendant: A bright trunk of electricity rises at the selected location and branches into a vast suspended tree. Bolts descend from its branch tips in marked clusters, while smaller arcs flow back up the trunk. The tree then folds its branches inward, releasing a final root-like wave across the ground.
heartbeat — LI-U10 Heartbeat of the Storm: A large charged core follows above the pet for a limited duration (~6 s), enclosed by several rotating broken rings. Each visible contraction sends branching strikes toward nearby enemies. With the final beat, the rings open and release a broad radial discharge, after which the core breaks into slow-falling, extinguishing sparks.
