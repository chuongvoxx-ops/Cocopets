# WIND (keys in ELEM_MOVES.wind, in this order)
Vocabulary: braided air currents, sweeping ribbons, pressure waves, spiral paths, suspended debris, vortices and sudden release.
Colours: C.col mint-green air (#8FE8C8), C.core pale (#F2FFF9); use translucent whites, pale mint and a little dust (sand/leaf flecks) to show depth. Wind is mostly transparent: ribbons with bright edges, pressure rings as refraction-like double rings.

## STRONG
silkstream — W-S01 Silkstream Cutter: Two pale air ribbons braid together ahead of the pet, then unfurl into a travelling crescent. Loose dust follows the twisting ribbon edges, revealing its depth. The crescent pierces a line of enemies before separating into thin, curling threads.
courier — W-S02 Spiral Courier: A compact corkscrew gust races toward the target, carrying tiny leaf-shaped air particles. It curves gently toward its destination and delivers several rapid hits on arrival. Its spiral loosens into an expanding helix before vanishing.
updraft — W-S03 Updraft Crown: A ring of narrow gusts erupts beneath the selected area, curving inward at their tips like a crown. Susceptible enemies are briefly lifted or staggered (H.lift). The gusts then peel outward, leaving descending feathers of pale air.
shears — W-S04 Crosswind Shears: Two broad currents sweep diagonally across the aimed zone. Their crossing point produces a concentrated pressure burst with a distinct circular ripple. Enemies hit by both currents take the full combination, rewarding precise placement.
pearl — W-S05 Vacuum Pearl: A clear, compressed air sphere travels slowly forward, distorting particles around its surface. It briefly pulls nearby susceptible enemies as it passes (H.pull), then pops into an inward-folding pressure ring. Sparse dust streaks show the pull without filling the screen.
galestair — W-S06 Gale Stair: Three rising gust platforms appear successively along the aimed path, each striking a slightly wider area. The pet performs a quick forward flourish as the sequence advances. Curved air trails connect the steps, which collapse into soft descending wisps.
featherstorm — W-S07 Featherstorm Fan: Thin feather-shaped blades of compressed air spread in a fan. Each rotates around its long axis and leaves a short translucent wake. The outer blades curve inward slightly, making the attack effective against a nearby group while preserving a readable cone.
zephyr — W-S08 Returning Zephyr: A flattened ring of wind travels outward and curves back toward the pet. Its outward pass deals a broad sweep; its return creates a narrower, stronger cut. Players can reposition to alter the return path through enemies (the return homes on the pet's current position).
needle — W-S09 Pressure Needle: Air gathers into a visibly compressed point in front of the pet. A brief release fires a fast piercing lance, followed by delayed dust ripples along its route. The impact blooms into a small transparent umbrella-shaped pressure wave.
tailwind — W-S10 Tailwind Sweep: The pet spins, sending a low wind crescent around itself and outward. Nearby enemies take a sweeping hit while a short-lived current follows the pet's movement. Movement benefit through the existing buff: `H.g.buff.haste = Math.max(H.g.buff.haste || 0, 2.5)` (the game's haste buff: 30% faster steps and quicker Normal attacks). The following current ticks damage round the pet for 1.5 s.

## ULTIMATES
skypalace — W-U01 Palace of the Sky: Broad ribbons rise into a floating pavilion of moving air above the target area. Its translucent arches rotate, then unfold into sweeping currents that strike the ground in sequence. The pavilion finally opens like an umbrella, releasing an expanding pressure wave and a descending shower of delicate air fragments.
migration — W-U02 Thousand-Wing Migration: A flock of abstract wind-bird silhouettes forms behind the pet, each built from a few curved ribbons. They spiral overhead and dive through enemies in coordinated waves. The flock merges into one enormous sweeping bird shape for the finishing pass, then separates into fading feather trails.
tempest — W-U03 Eye of the Tempest: Several small vortices travel around a clear central eye, drawing susceptible enemies toward a marked ring. Their paths tighten while suspended debris rises through layered spirals. The vortices merge into a broad storm wall, then collapse outward in one final circular strike, leaving the centre visible throughout.
loom — W-U04 Loom of the Heavens: Long air ribbons stretch between floating pressure nodes across the selected zone. The ribbons weave over and under one another, creating a moving three-dimensional lattice. Successive rows sweep downward as cutting currents before the weave unravels into a single forward surge of braided wind.
severance — W-U05 Horizon Severance: A thin curved line of compressed air appears across the battlefield. Multiple translucent crescents stack behind it at different heights, slowly aligning. They release as staggered travelling blades, each wider than the last. The final blade leaves a briefly suspended curtain of drifting dust along its path.
dandelion — W-U06 Dandelion Cyclone: A luminous seed-like air core rises, surrounded by hundreds of lightweight wind tufts (dozens, thinned by H.n). The tufts scatter into clearly grouped spirals, each tracing an orbit before converging on enemies. A final expanding gust sends the remaining tufts across the ground, where they dissolve into curling white threads.
giantbow — W-U07 Invisible Giant's Bow: Air bends into a colossal curved bow above the pet, its outline revealed by streaming dust and ribbon tension. Several spiral strands form the drawn string. Release launches a vast corkscrew lance through the aimed corridor, followed by delayed pressure bursts travelling along the same route.
carousel — W-U08 Skyfall Carousel: Six funnel-shaped currents descend around the target zone and rotate like a slow carousel. Their bases sweep distinct ground paths while their upper ribbons interlace overhead. They accelerate, converge at the centre, and release a towering updraft that breaks into an outward rain of harmless wind fragments.
breath — W-U09 Breath Between Mountains: Two monumental walls of translucent air form beside a wide corridor. Loose particles accelerate between them as pressure builds. A sustained gale then tears down the corridor in pulsing waves, finishing with a rolling cylindrical gust. The walls peel away into enormous, fading ribbon curls.
hurricane — W-U10 Crownless Hurricane: A ring of wind blades forms around the pet and follows its movement for a limited duration (~6 s). The blades rise and fall in staggered orbits, periodically launching outward crescents. At the end, all remaining blades expand into a broad circular sweep, leaving a calm pocket around the pet.
