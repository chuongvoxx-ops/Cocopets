Annihilation — The Supreme Dark Ultimate

Create a spectacular Dark ultimate with six glowing purple magic circles, six ascending energy missiles, an enormous overhead convergence, and a devastating downward strike. Give every phase detailed animation, rich purple shading, luminous geometric patterns, and carefully directed particles.

1. Six lights awaken

Six concentrated purple lights erupt across the battlefield. Each begins as a narrow pillar with a brilliant violet centre and dark, curling energy around its edges.

The bases spread outward, drawing one giant magic circle and five smaller circles of visibly different sizes. Arrange the smaller circles around the central one, leaving enough separation to appreciate each design.

Draw the circles progressively: outer arcs trace themselves across the ground, concentric rings rotate in opposite directions, and gothic glyphs illuminate in sequence. Fine spokes connect the rings to bright central seals.

Purple sparks crawl around the inscriptions and jump between their edges. Small fragments of shadow rise above the circles, suspended by the gathering power.

2. Six missiles erupt

Exactly one missile bursts upward from each circle. The central circle launches the largest; the five surrounding circles launch five smaller missiles.

Give them brilliant violet cores, deep-purple outer layers, pointed leading shapes, and long spiralling tails. Their launch briefly floods each circle with light.

The missiles rise, then curve toward a shared convergence point above the battlefield. Their paths should be smooth and dramatic: the central missile climbs through the middle while the others spiral around it on different arcs.

Their lingering trails briefly create a colossal three-dimensional ritual pattern connecting the ground to the sky.

3. The sky fills with purple light

As each missile reaches the convergence point, it feeds an expanding sphere of energy. Every arrival sends a visible ripple across the sphere, making it larger and more turbulent.

The completed light should feel immense: a luminous lavender core surrounded by swirling violet currents, counter-rotating energy bands, and a darker translucent outer shell.

Keep its internal layers visible through the glow. Sparks escaping its surface curve back inward.

Hold it briefly at full charge. All six circles pulse together, feeding their remaining light upward. The sphere compresses slightly as its underside becomes intensely bright.

4. The downward strike

The accumulated energy shoots straight down toward the giant circle.

Make the strike thick, concentrated, and forceful, with a violet-white leading tip and twisting purple streams behind it. The overhead sphere visibly empties into the strike.

Immediately before impact, the giant circle’s inscriptions flare outward from its centre.

5. The annihilation blast

Begin with a tightly concentrated violet-white impact flash, followed by an enormous expanding blast shell.

A broad purple shockwave races across the ground, throwing out dark fragments, low rolling mist, and luminous pressure streaks. Behind it, a towering column rises into a mushroom-like crown of purple-black magical energy, giving the explosion its nuclear-scale silhouette.

Illuminate the cloud from within. Its underside glows violet while darker folds roll over its upper surface. Fine sparks flicker through the expanding layers.

The six circles fracture into glowing segments. Their glyphs lift from the ground and disintegrate as temporary purple cracks spread beneath the blast.

Let the aftermath linger beautifully: falling fragments, drifting ash, curling purple clouds, and a slowly extinguishing central glow.
