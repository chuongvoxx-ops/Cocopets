# Wind ultimate: Breath of the Wild — the user's spec (verbatim, 2026-11-07)

Row: key `breathofthewild` replaces Wind `breath` (Breath Between Mountains), cost 100, cd 120, glyph 'leaf'.

Create a Wind ultimate called Breath of the Wild. The pet summons a lush, flower-filled sanctuary where gentle currents continuously regenerate allied pets’ HP.

Active duration: 20 seconds. Cooldown: 120 seconds. Keep these values at every skill level. Begin the healing duration when the sanctuary activates and the cooldown when the skill is cast.

Formation

The pet lowers its body slightly as a soft mint-green light gathers beneath it. A circular breeze spreads across the ground, carrying tiny pink petals, pale green motes and shimmering pollen.

As the breeze travels outward, fresh grass sprouts along its path. Small stems emerge, leaves unfurl, and flower buds open in a rolling sequence from the centre toward the perimeter.

Grow clusters of pink, white and pale lavender flowers at varied heights. Leave natural gaps between them so the battlefield remains readable. The completed area should resemble a magical meadow stirred by a constant, beautiful breeze.

Use simple three-dimensional stems, leaves and layered petal meshes with enough thickness and shading to remain attractive from the gameplay camera.

The living wind field

Create several broad, translucent ribbons of mint and pearly green wind that circulate through the meadow. Some skim the grass; others rise in gentle arcs before curling back toward the ground.

Give these currents different speeds and heights. Flowers bend as each gust reaches them, then recover with a slight delayed sway. Petals lift from scattered clusters, spiral through the air and drift down elsewhere.

Keep the centre open and clear. Define the sanctuary’s boundary with a soft moving wreath of petals and a narrow green ground glow.

Use deep green shading at the bases of the plants, lighter leaf edges, softly luminous flower centres and restrained pearly highlights. Concentrate bloom around drifting pollen and wind streaks while keeping the flowers’ shapes visible.

Healing animation and behaviour

All living allied pets inside the sanctuary regenerate HP throughout its 20-second active duration, including the caster. Use a proposed healing interval of once per second, with the amount configured through the game’s healing system.

Every healing pulse sends a gentle green ripple through the meadow. Nearby flower centres brighten, releasing small lights that curve toward the pets receiving healing.

When these lights arrive, a delicate spiral of mint light rises around each recipient, carrying a few petals and sparkling pollen particles. Make this response visible enough that players can recognise when their pets are being healed.

The sanctuary remains at its cast position. Pets begin receiving healing when they enter and stop receiving it when they leave. It restores HP without reviving fallen teammates.

Level progression

* Level 1: a complete flowering meadow with flowing wind ribbons, gently swaying plants and clearly animated healing pulses.
* Level 4: increase healing strength and area. Add taller flower clusters, richer foliage and a second layer of elevated wind currents. Flower illumination travels across the meadow in soft waves during each healing pulse.
* Level 7 Celestial: create an expansive enchanted meadow with layered blossoms and elegant streams of airborne petals. Several translucent wind arches curve over the sanctuary, forming an open canopy. Each healing pulse briefly traces a beautiful flower pattern across the ground before breaking into lights that flow toward allied pets.

Increase visual richness through plant variety, coordinated motion and layered currents. Preserve the 20-second duration and two-minute cooldown.

Expiration

During the final seconds, the wind currents gradually rise and the flower centres soften.

At exactly 20 seconds, healing stops. The flowers then release their remaining petals, fold gently and dissolve into green motes. Grass fades in a rolling wave while the last wind ribbons unwind upward.

Treat the meadow as temporary magical vegetation. It should conform to the battlefield surface, avoid blocking movement and disappear completely after the effect ends.
