# ICE (keys in ELEM_MOVES.ice, in this order)
Vocabulary: crystalline growth, frost patterns, unfolding formations, suspended shards, snow spirals and cascading shatters. Colours: pale cyan-white glass (#9FE0F8, #DDF6FF, #FFFFFF) with deep blue shadows (#2F80B7, #1B4F7A) and sapphire accents (#3B7BFF). Ice is sharp, faceted and translucent: H.crystal, H.glass, hard angular polygons, frost branches as thin white lines, snow as 'snow' particles. Keep it distinct from Water (no wobble, no foam). Freeze/chill through dmgO({ freeze, chill }) and H.bind/H.slow; bosses only chill.
The game's existing Blizzard Lotus (a 5★ star move) is the quality benchmark and is NOT one of these; do not reuse its petal motif.

## STRONG
antler — I-S01 Crystal Antler: Branching ice antlers grow rapidly from the ground along a short forward path. Frost travels from their bases to their sharp tips before each formation shatters outward. The branching geometry creates a distinctive close-range piercing attack.
skate — I-S02 Glacier Skate: A smooth ice blade slides low across the battlefield, leaving a narrow frosted track. Its edges shed fine white powder while its centre remains glass-clear. At the endpoint, it tilts upward and fractures into a compact shard fan.
snowbell — I-S03 Snowglass Bell: A thin bell-shaped ice shell forms over the selected position. A bright frost ring travels up its surface before it cracks. The break releases a short radial burst of curved fragments and a soft halo of snow.
frostloom — I-S04 Frostneedle Loom: Several long ice needles appear in two staggered rows ahead of the pet. They fire alternately, creating a woven-looking pattern of crossing trails. Each impact releases fine frost branches that quickly retract and vanish.
wintermoth — I-S05 Winter Moth: A small crystalline moth unfolds from angular ice plates and flutters toward the target. Its wings shed powdery snow on each beat. On arrival, it folds sharply into a piercing shard and bursts into symmetrical wing-shaped fragments.
rimecoil — I-S06 Rimecoil: A narrow ribbon of frost winds around the selected enemy from the ground upward. Small crystal plates grow along the spiral, briefly slowing the target where supported (H.slow). The plates then snap outward in a tightly bounded damage burst.
pendulum — I-S07 Icicle Pendulum: A long icicle forms above and beside the pet, suspended from a curved frost trail. It swings forward through a broad arc and shatters at its lowest point. The attack combines a readable sweep with a compact finishing splash of shards.
dominoes — I-S08 Polar Dominoes: A line of upright ice plates rises toward the aimed position. The nearest plate tips, triggering a forward chain of shattering impacts. Each plate briefly reflects the next before breaking, making the sequence easy to follow.
hoarfrost — I-S09 Hoarfrost Halo: A ring of thin ice blades rotates once around the pet, then expands outward. Its blades tumble slowly along their individual axes while snow threads connect the ring. The attack clears nearby enemies and leaves a faint circular frost trace.
hailshot — I-S10 Sapphire Hailshot: A dense blue crystal forms between three orbiting ice chips and fires as a heavy projectile. On impact, its outer layers peel off into separate shards before the core bursts. Strong single-target damage with a small secondary splash.

## ULTIMATES
stillwinter — I-U01 Palace of Still Winter: Transparent ice walls rise around the target zone, etched with rapidly spreading frost patterns. Tall spires emerge at their intersections and fire crystal lances inward. The palace fractures in a wave from its outer walls to the centre, ending in a towering shard eruption and a slow fall of glittering snow.
aurorastag — I-U02 Aurora Stag Procession: A monumental stag silhouette forms from branching ice and translucent plates. It runs through the aimed corridor, its hooves producing separate frost impacts. Its antlers grow broader with each stride, then sweep forward in a final crystal arc. The body disassembles into prismatic fragments beneath a faint aurora-like trail.
frozenorrery — I-U03 Frozen Orrery: Nested ice rings rotate around a suspended blue crystal core. Tiny snow particles flow along each orbit before the rings detach and sweep downward through the target zone. The core then splits into multiple long icicles that fall sequentially, leaving an intricate lattice of fading frost lines.
snowglass — I-U04 Cathedral of Snowglass: Tall transparent panels assemble into a vaulted ice structure above the selected area. Frost spreads across each panel in different branching patterns. Resonant pulses travel through the structure, triggering repeated damage below, before the vault breaks into a carefully staged cascade of large shards and fine snow.
whitecomet — I-U05 Crown of the White Comet: A large ice comet forms above the pet, wrapped in spinning crystal fins. It accelerates toward the selected area, trailing a long ribbon of powder and sparkling fragments. On landing, its fins unfold into a radial crown before shattering outward; the central comet produces a final vertical frost burst.
hourglass — I-U06 Winter's Hourglass: Two cones of suspended snow form a giant hourglass above the target zone. Fine crystals stream through its narrow centre, falling as repeated icy strikes. As the upper half empties, the structure rotates and collapses downward, releasing an expanding wave of ice plates and gently drifting powder.
sevenfold — I-U07 Sevenfold Glacier: Seven curved glacier slabs rise successively along a wide forward corridor. Each slab slides ahead and fractures, feeding shards into the next formation. The final slab grows into an enormous translucent wave, folds over, and breaks into a cascading field of ice with a clearly marked final impact.
mirrorlabyrinth — I-U08 Polar Mirror Labyrinth: Tall mirrored ice prisms appear in a spacious pattern around enemies. Frost lances ricochet visibly between their surfaces, producing a sequence of crossing attacks. The prisms then rotate inward, refract one final converging beam of cold, and collapse into neat piles of dissolving crystal fragments.
snowfall — I-U09 Thousand-Wing Snowfall: A flock of crystalline moths emerges from a rising column of snow. They orbit at different heights, then dive in grouped waves, each moth becoming a narrow shard before impact. The remaining flock forms a huge pair of ice wings that closes over the target area and shatters.
absolutewinter — I-U10 Heart of Absolute Winter: A faceted ice heart grows above the selected point, beating through visible pulses of pale blue light. Each pulse sends frost across the ground and raises a ring of crystals. The heart opens along geometric seams, revealing a brilliant core that releases the final wave before everything dissolves into fine snow.
