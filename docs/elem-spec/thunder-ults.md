# LIGHTNING ULTIMATES (the user's design, verbatim; they replace the ten old thunder Ultimates)
Keys in ELEM_MOVES.thunder.ult, in order: flash, thunderstorm, widepressure, ragnarok, supreme, glory, heavensbow, lotus,
butterfly, worldsplitter. "Level 1 / 4 / 7" = the pet's stage C.evo (1 Hatchling, 4 Adept, 7 Celestial); stages 2–3
build toward level 4 and 5–6 toward level 7.

These are written for six-star Cocopets, with each skill progressing from level 1 to level 7 Celestial.

The shared visual direction is white-hot lightning cores, saturated electric-blue and violet layers, selective golden
highlights, flowing trails, and clearly animated structures inside the glow. Every ultimate has a deliberate buildup,
powerful release, and beautiful fading animation.

1. Lightning Flash
The pet crouches as electricity gathers along its body. Its markings illuminate, sparks pull toward its feet, and its silhouette becomes a brilliant electric outline.
It launches through enemies in successive lightning dashes. Each dash leaves a tapered plasma ribbon: white at the leading edge, cyan through the centre, and violet along the fringes. At every change of direction, a recognisable afterimage of the pet briefly remains, already leaning into its next movement. Sparks spray backward as it accelerates.
Level 1: Four sharp dashes, with a stronger flare on the fourth contact.
Level 4: Eight dashes. Two fine electrical filaments spiral around each main trail. The pet circles the enemy group before cutting through it from alternating angles.
Level 7 Celestial: Sixteen dashes performed in four accelerating bursts of four. Their trails briefly reveal an intricate luminous pattern across the battlefield. The sixteenth dash becomes a long, spear-shaped streak. On its final contact, the remaining trail fragments race toward the pet and disappear into its outline as it lands amid backward-streaming sparks.

2. Thunderstorm
The pet raises its head as a translucent storm canopy gathers above the target area. Violet currents move inside the clouds, illuminating them from within.
Before each strike, a thin electrical tendril rises from the ground. A much larger bolt descends to meet it, splitting into angular branches before striking. Each impact creates a low electrical shockwave, crawling ground arcs, and a fading violet impression of the bolt.
The strikes build in rhythm: measured impacts first, then increasingly rapid exchanges across the battlefield.
Level 1: Six lightning strikes, followed by one large finishing burst.
Level 4: Ten strikes. Bolts land in alternating pairs, while luminous currents connect recent impact points.
Level 7 Celestial: Sixteen strikes in four escalating sequences. After the sixteenth, the clouds briefly open around a brilliant storm eye. One colossal, heavily branching bolt falls through it, releasing a broad electric corona across the ground. The storm then unravels into fine, descending threads of light.

3. Sparkling Wide Pressure
Electrical beads gather around the pet, stretch into circular rims, and tilt upright. Each becomes a rolling thunder wheel with a churning spherical core, curved spokes, and a brilliant outer edge.
The wheels launch in a staggered fan. Their rims visibly rotate against the ground, spraying sparks from their contact points. They bank into sweeping turns and leave twin violet tracks behind them. When a wheel passes through an enemy, its spokes bend backward with the impact before snapping into shape.
Level 1: Four wheels, each with one luminous rim and four curved spokes.
Level 4: Ten wheels weave through the enemy formation along alternating curved routes. Counter-rotating inner rings give them more depth and movement.
Level 7 Celestial: Sixteen wheels launch in two flowing waves of eight. Each has three nested rotating rims, petal-shaped electrical spokes, and golden leading edges. During their final second, they tighten their turns toward enemies, then collapse individually into spinning discs of sparks. Their staggered disappearance creates a travelling finish across the arena.

4. Ragnarok
The pet braces as charged fragments lift from the ground. High above the target, a spectral handle forms, followed by an enormous hammerhead assembling from angular plates of lightning.
Electricity writhes inside the translucent head around a white-hot core. The hammer pauses overhead, tilted backward, while small arcs reach down toward its landing point.
It swings downward with heavy acceleration. Long electrical ribbons trail behind it. At impact, the light compresses beneath its striking face before erupting into a thick radial shockwave and branching ground discharges.
Level 1: One giant spirit hammer, one electric explosion, and 10 seconds of paralysis on susceptible enemies.
Level 4: A broader hammerhead, a more elaborate internal thunder core, increased damage, and a wider impact area.
Level 7 Celestial: A monumental hammer with floating outer plates surrounding a rotating core. During its descent, those plates close together into one massive striking face. Its impact sends a crown of lightning across the floor, followed by an electrical dome that breaks into descending sparks. The hammer briefly remains embedded in its glowing impact mark before disintegrating.
The attack remains one hammer strike. Damage and area increase; the base paralysis duration stays at 10 seconds.

5. Supreme Thunder
The pet summons living lightning spheres around the player for 10 seconds. Each sphere contains a brilliant core surrounded by curved electrical filaments rotating at different angles.
Their orbits rise and fall gently, letting them pass above and below one another. When an enemy approaches, a sphere leans outward, stretches a short electrical tether behind itself, then darts toward its target. It delivers a close-range zap and returns along a smooth curved path.
The animation alternates between graceful orbiting and sudden, aggressive lunges.
Level 1: Four spheres attack in a clear rotating rhythm.
Level 4: Eight spheres occupy two tilted orbital paths. They perform staggered paired attacks and leave spiral wakes.
Level 7 Celestial: Twelve spheres travel along three orbital paths of four, forming a moving sculpture of electricity around the player. Attacking spheres develop golden comet tails while the others retain their cyan-violet glow. At the end, all twelve briefly align around the player, perform their final coordinated zaps, and unravel into curling electrical filaments.

6. Edge of Glory
Two immense wings made from lightning blades unfold behind the pet.
Each blade resembles a long crescent feather: a razor-bright golden edge, translucent cyan body, and ragged violet fringe. The pet turns its body, and the wings follow its movement, sweeping across the enemy formation.
Every swing leaves a curved sheet of glowing air that hangs briefly before separating into fine electrical strands. Smaller blades follow the leading edge with a slight delay, creating beautiful layered fans.
Level 1: Two single-blade wings perform a broad opening sweep, then cross in front of the pet to release a travelling X-shaped slash.
Level 4: Six blade-feathers form the wings. Two alternating sweeps precede the crossed finishing attack.
Level 7 Celestial: Twelve blade-feathers unfold from base to tip. Three increasingly powerful sweeps lead into the finale: the wings spread to their full span, draw together into two enormous edges, then snap forward. A huge crossed lightning cut travels through the battlefield, with gold and violet filaments twisting along its edges. A delicate outline of the wings remains behind the pet, dissolving feather by feather.

7. Heaven's Bow
Two streams of electricity rise beside the pet and form a gigantic floating longbow. Its curved limbs contain layered currents, and a thin white lightning string connects their tips.
As the pet draws backward into a braced pose, the bow bends. Lightning arrows materialise along the string, spreading into a luminous fan. Each arrow has a crystalline electrical head and three streaming filaments in place of feathers.
The smaller arrows release in a rapid sequence, followed by a second draw that concentrates the remaining charge into one immense piercing arrow.
Level 1: Three smaller arrows, followed by one large piercing shot.
Level 4: Five smaller arrows. A second glowing contour forms around the bow, while currents visibly travel from its tips into the final arrow.
Level 7 Celestial: Nine arrows fire in three groups of three. For the finishing shot, three layered bow contours align, three electrical strings become one, and a huge arrowhead assembles from rotating lightning facets. Its release visibly recoils through the bow and pet. The arrow pierces the enemy line with a braided electrical wake, while the bow disassembles from its tips inward.

8. Thunder Lotus
The pet sends a brilliant electrical seed into the ground beneath the enemies. Branching veins spread across the surface, then rise and curl inward to form a closed lotus bud.
Its petals are dimensional ribbons of electricity, with white-violet veins, cyan edges, and small arcs jumping between their ribs. The flower opens in a deliberate spiral. Each unfolding layer sends a pulse of electricity from the centre toward its tips.
Electrical beads race along the petal edges and fling outward as sparks.
Level 1: One ring of six petals opens around the enemies and releases two damage pulses.
Level 4: Two staggered rings of nine petals open in opposite directions. Three pulses travel through the flower, briefly connecting neighbouring petals with lightning bridges.
Level 7 Celestial: Three rings of twelve petals unfold into a towering inner bloom above a wide outer flower. Four pulses climb through its structure. On the final pulse, every layer opens to its full extent, sending a luminous wave through all the petals simultaneously. The entire lotus then peels apart into curling electrical strands, leaving a delicate glowing flower pattern on the floor.

9. Butterfly Tempest
Violet sparks lift from the pet and split into razor-winged lightning butterflies. Each has four clearly animated wing lobes, brilliant electrical veins, translucent surfaces, and sharp sparkling edges.
They circle the pet in a rising spiral before peeling away toward enemies. Their movement follows a distinct rhythm: flutter, bank, accelerate, strike. During an attack, their wings fold backward into narrow lightning blades. After cutting through the target, their wings reform on the other side.
Level 1: Six butterflies attack, then combine into one larger butterfly for a finishing pass.
Level 4: Twelve butterflies launch in staggered pairs. Their trails curve around each other, and the combined butterfly gains a second translucent wing outline that follows its movements.
Level 7 Celestial: Twenty-four butterflies attack in four coordinated waves. Their returning trails then draw the silhouette of an enormous butterfly in the air before its body appears. Its wings open in two stages, revealing intricate branching lightning veins. One powerful wingbeat launches it through the enemy formation, trailing long violet streamers. It dissolves into slowly turning luminous wing fragments.

10. Worldsplitter
The pet strikes the ground. A needle-thin violet fracture shoots forward, branching through the enemy formation as electricity pulses beneath its surface.
After a short anticipation, the fracture ignites from the pet outward. Each segment erupts into a vertical sheet of lightning, creating a travelling electrical curtain that tears across the battlefield like an enormous opening zipper.
The leading edge burns white. Behind it, the towering sheets soften into translucent violet and collapse into sparks. Arcs leap across the opening, while small suspended fragments lift along its sides.
Level 1: One main fault with two short side branches.
Level 4: A longer central fault with four branches that ignite alternately left and right. Twin electrical sheets rise along its edges.
Level 7 Celestial: One great central fault with six sweeping branches. Light first races beneath every path, revealing the complete shape. The main eruption then advances, igniting each branch as it reaches its junction. At the far end, the curtain rises into a colossal forked crest and folds forward in a final discharge. The fault seals from the far end back toward the pet, leaving floating sparks and fading violet traces.
