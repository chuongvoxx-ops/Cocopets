# LIGHT (keys in ELEM_MOVES.light, in this order)
Vocabulary: prismatic structures, intersecting rays, radiant geometry, luminous constructs, rotating halos and cascading brilliance. Keep Light distinct from Lightning: no jagged arcs; Light is smooth rays, planes, halos, stained glass, warm gold-white (#FFF0A0 / #FFFFFF) with prismatic rainbow flecks (pinks, cyans) where rays refract. No full-screen flashes beyond short H.X.flash.

## STRONG
prismpetal — L-S01 Prism Petal: Three crystal-like light plates unfold ahead of the pet and redirect a short beam through their angled surfaces. The refracted rays fan across nearby enemies. Each plate dissolves from its edges into rainbow flecks after the final ray passes.
dawnspear — L-S02 Dawnstep Spear: A luminous spear assembles from sliding geometric segments and fires along the aimed direction. It leaves a thin golden line suspended briefly behind it. On impact, the spear opens into a four-pointed starburst rather than a conventional explosion.
halo — L-S03 Halo Ricochet: A small rotating halo launches toward an enemy and rebounds between nearby valid targets. Each contact briefly imprints a luminous ring before it fractures into motes. The halo becomes thinner with each rebound and finally dissolves into a curved light trail.
lanternfall — L-S04 Lanternfall: Three floating lantern-like constructs appear above the selected area. They open their lower panels in sequence, releasing narrow columns of warm light. The lanterns collapse into sparks after firing, giving the attack a readable three-beat rhythm.
stitch — L-S05 Radiant Stitch: A needle of light pierces forward, leaving several suspended luminous points along its route. A thread snaps between those points a moment later, causing a second line hit. The completed thread gently curves upward and fades.
mirrorwing — L-S06 Mirrorwing Sweep: Two translucent mirrored wings unfold beside the pet and sweep forward. Their surfaces catch moving bands of colour while their edges deliver a broad cone strike. They close into a narrow shard that dissolves above the pet.
sunseed — L-S07 Sunseed: A small golden seed lodges at the selected location and gathers nearby motes. It sprouts into a short radiant stalk, then bursts into a hemispherical flash of pointed rays. The visible growth provides clear warning before the delayed area damage.
lucentwheel — L-S08 Lucent Wheel: A segmented disc of light rolls upright along the ground. Its segments brighten sequentially, producing a strobing sense of rotation without full-screen flashes. On its final contact, the disc unfolds flat into a brief circular light field.
chime — L-S09 Cathedral Chime: A translucent bell-shaped construct appears above the pet. Its silent visual swing is followed by a luminous sound-like ring that expands across nearby enemies. Small suspended prisms vibrate and fade along the ring's edge.
starpoint — L-S10 Starpoint Volley: Five bright points form a small constellation around the pet's head. They fire precise lances one at a time toward the chosen target, with subtle connecting lines showing the remaining pattern. The last point bursts into a concentrated star-shaped impact.

## ULTIMATES
firstdawn — L-U01 Cathedral of First Dawn: Tall translucent window panels rise around the target zone, filled with slowly moving geometric colour patterns. Rays pass through them and sweep across the ground in timed lanes. The panels then turn toward the centre, combining their light into a radiant pillar that breaks into floating stained-glass fragments.
orrery — L-U02 Celestial Orrery: Nested golden rings assemble around a brilliant floating core. Small luminous satellites travel along different orbital planes, firing downward rays as they align with enemies. The rings gradually flatten into one vast halo, which descends as an expanding final strike before dissolving into fine ascending motes.
lanterns — L-U03 Thousand Lantern Ascension: Glowing lantern constructs emerge across the selected area and rise at different speeds. Each releases a downward pulse as it opens. Their light trails connect into a vaulted canopy overhead, which gathers into a central sunburst and showers the battlefield with slowly fading golden squares.
seraphic — L-U04 Seraphic Refraction: A many-winged geometric light construct forms behind the pet, with each wing made from separate translucent planes. The wings rotate and redirect successive beams toward the aimed area. They finally fold forward together, producing a wide prismatic sweep and a wake of delicate, feather-shaped light fragments.
guilloche — L-U05 Daybreak Guilloche: Fine luminous lines draw a complex looping pattern across the ground. Matching three-dimensional arcs rise from selected intersections, forming a rotating sculpture of light. Damage pulses travel visibly along the pattern before the entire design lifts and contracts into a brilliant central detonation.
mirrors — L-U06 Palace of Mirrors: A circle of tall floating mirrors appears around the target zone. A light lance ricochets between them in a readable sequence, crossing enemies along each route. The mirrors then face downward and fire together, after which their surfaces ripple and separate into harmless silver-gold flakes.
solarengine — L-U07 Solar Bloom Engine: A mechanical-looking sun made of luminous plates forms above the pet. Its plates slide apart in successive layers, exposing an increasingly bright core. It releases several broad forward rays, then ejects its outer plates as sweeping crescents. The remaining core fades into a small floating spark.
verdict — L-U08 Constellation Verdict: Stars appear above individual enemies, then connect through a suspended constellation. Each connection sends a pulse down to its marked target. A larger central star forms after the sequence and drops a broad pillar into the selected area, scattering fine constellation lines like broken luminous threads.
heavenriver — L-U09 River of Heaven: A winding elevated ribbon of golden light pours across the battlefield along the aimed route. Small stars flow within it like stones in clear water. The ribbon dips to strike the ground in successive sections, then rises and unfurls into a broad, shimmering curtain that dissolves overhead.
unsettingsun — L-U10 Crown of the Unsetting Sun: A vast segmented crown rotates above the selected zone, casting moving spokes of light onto the floor. Its segments descend one at a time as precise radiant strikes. The remaining central halo expands into a final wave, while the crown's outline lingers briefly as a delicate golden afterimage.
