# WATER (keys in ELEM_MOVES.water, in this order)
Vocabulary: coiling streams, suspended droplets, flowing sculptures, tidal folds, whirlpools, spray and refractive surfaces. Keep Water distinct from Ice: water is translucent deep blue to cyan (#1F5FA8, #4FA8F0, #8FE3F0) with white foam, caustic highlight bands and bright specular points; it flows and wobbles (sinusoidal edges), never crystalline or sharp. Use H.glass for bodies of water, H.sphere with a translucent body for droplets/pearls, foam as white sparks and 'bubble' particles. Wet status comes from the element itself via dmgO.

## STRONG
ribbonfin — WA-S01 Ribbonfin: A translucent water ribbon curls into a fish-like shape and darts forward. Its body flexes naturally while droplets peel from the tail. On impact, it wraps once around the target and breaks into a circular splash.
tideglass — WA-S02 Tideglass Orb: A clear water sphere rolls slowly through enemies, its surface carrying rotating bands of reflected light. Each contact sends small ripples through its volume. At maximum range, it stretches upward and collapses into a short radial splash.
crescentsurf — WA-S03 Crescent Surf: The pet releases a low crescent-shaped wave that widens as it travels. White foam gathers along its leading edge, clearly showing the damage boundary. The wave curls sideways at the end and dissolves into scattered droplets.
rainneedle — WA-S04 Rainneedle Fan: A suspended water sheet forms briefly ahead of the pet, then separates into a fan of narrow droplets. The droplets accelerate into piercing rain needles. Their impacts produce tiny crown-shaped splashes and short-lived wet glints.
whirlpool — WA-S05 Whirlpool Step: A shallow spiral pool appears at the selected position and rotates inward. It pulls susceptible enemies slightly toward its centre (H.pull) while dealing repeated damage. The centre then rises as a small water column and collapses outward.
bubblesnare — WA-S06 Bubble Snare: A large iridescent bubble travels toward the target and encloses a small area on contact. Susceptible enemies are slowed inside its wobbling surface (H.slow). The bubble contracts visibly before popping into a clearly bounded spray burst.
waterwheel — WA-S07 Waterwheel Strike: Curved water blades assemble into an upright rotating wheel. It surges forward, striking several times as its blades contact enemies. The wheel loses cohesion gradually, becoming a rolling arc of foam before breaking apart.
twinundertow — WA-S08 Twin Undertow: Two narrow streams travel outward on either side of the aimed line, then curve toward each other. Their meeting point creates a concentrated upward splash. Enemies caught between the streams receive the strongest overlap.
pearlburst — WA-S09 Pearlburst Volley: Three dense water pearls hover beside the pet and fire sequentially. Each carries smaller droplets in orbit, which scatter outward on impact. The final pearl produces a slightly broader splash to finish the barrage.
basin — WA-S10 Falling Basin: A shallow floating bowl of water forms above the selected area and tips. Its contents pour down as a brief concentrated waterfall, followed by a spreading ground splash. The bowl's rim dissolves into descending droplets after the pour.

## ULTIMATES
deeppalace — WA-U01 Palace of the Deep: Translucent water columns rise around the target zone and connect through flowing overhead arches. Currents travel visibly inside the architecture before pouring downward in sequential waterfalls. The entire palace then folds into a central wave that bursts outward, leaving suspended droplets that fall gently after the damaging phase.
koispiral — WA-U02 Celestial Koi Spiral: Two enormous water koi form from coiling streams and circle the target area at different heights. Their fins trail translucent sheets and pearl-like droplets. They dive through enemies in alternating passes, then meet at the centre in a twisting water pillar that blooms into an expanding ring of spray.
moontide — WA-U03 Moon-Tide Observatory: A large suspended water sphere appears above the selected zone, surrounded by several orbital streams. The streams periodically descend as sweeping tidal arcs. The sphere gradually stretches into a vast droplet, falls into the centre, and rebounds into a hollow crown-shaped splash with clearly defined damaging edges.
leviathanribbon — WA-U04 Leviathan's Ribbon: A long aquatic serpent made from layered water ribbons coils behind the pet. It surges forward in three broad bends, each bend producing a ground-level wave. At the endpoint, it rises vertically and unravels into a descending spiral waterfall, scattering luminous droplets along the spiral's outer edge.
monsoon — WA-U05 Thousand-Pearl Monsoon: Pearl-like water spheres rise from the ground and hover above the target area in staggered layers. Each refracts a small moving highlight before falling in grouped volleys. The final cluster merges into a broad descending water curtain, followed by an outward surge of foam and slowly settling mist.
shellocean — WA-U06 Ocean in a Shell: A monumental translucent shell forms above the battlefield from curved water surfaces. It opens gradually, revealing a rotating miniature ocean inside. Waves spill from its edge in successive forward sweeps. The shell closes around the remaining water, then releases one concentrated surge before dissolving into pearlescent spray.
whirlgalaxy — WA-U07 Whirlpool Galaxy: Several small whirlpools orbit a larger central vortex, linked by narrow flowing streams. Their paths create a shifting, clearly marked damage pattern across the floor. The outer vortices spiral inward, stacking into a tall water helix that collapses into a broad final wave.
jellyfish — WA-U08 Jellyfish Aurora: Giant translucent jellyfish-like water constructs descend over the selected zone, their bells pulsing with soft cyan light. Flowing tendrils sweep marked lanes beneath them. The bells contract together, releasing downward water pulses, then rise and dissolve into long ribbons of droplets without implying an Ice or Lightning attack.
returningsea — WA-U09 Throne of the Returning Sea: A towering wave rises behind the pet, sculpted into layered terraces of moving water. The terraces release forward surges one after another, each overtaking the previous foam trail. The final wave curls overhead and breaks at the aimed point, leaving a spectacular hollow tunnel of water as it collapses.
rainmaker — WA-U10 Rainmaker's Orrery: Rotating water rings form around a floating central droplet. Smaller droplets transfer between the rings, growing as the structure turns. They fire along curved paths into the selected area, followed by the rings descending as successive circular splashes. The central droplet remains briefly before softly bursting into mist.
