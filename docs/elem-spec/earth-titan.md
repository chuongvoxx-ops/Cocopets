Create an Earth ultimate called Titan for my existing Cocopets game. It summons one enormous stone golem that fights alongside the pet for exactly 25 seconds.

Use the attached image as the visual reference. Build a complete, animated three-dimensional creature, including its sides, back, joints and undersides. Match the reference’s silhouette, proportions, stone construction and moss placement while fitting the game’s existing art style.

Appearance and proportions

Titan is a hulking, ancient stone guardian with an extremely broad upper body, enormous shoulders, oversized forearms and fists, and short, thick legs. Its posture leans slightly forward, with its arms hanging low and its heavy hands close to knee height.

Make it approximately three times the pet’s standing height as a starting scale, adjusted to suit the battlefield. Its width and mass should make it imposing without obscuring the entire combat area.

The body consists of irregular, interlocking boulders and thick stone plates. Use deliberate anatomical shapes: broad chest slabs, overlapping abdominal plates, large shoulder masses and reinforced knees. Give every major section visible thickness and deep gaps between adjoining stones.

Head and face

Place a relatively small head low between the shoulders, with almost no exposed neck. Build it from angular stone pieces with a heavy brow, recessed eye sockets, broad cheek plates and a thick, squared jaw.

Give it two small glowing white eyes with a faint green tint. The eyes should sit deep inside dark sockets, making its expression stern and ancient.

Use a simple recessed mouth seam and a rugged stone muzzle. Keep the face readable through its brow, eyes and jaw rather than adding intricate facial anatomy.

Shoulders, arms and hands

The shoulders are the largest masses after the torso: enormous overlapping boulders with broad flat faces, chipped corners and a few short, blunt stone projections.

The upper arms are thick, but the forearms become even larger toward the wrists. Construct each forearm from several long rock plates surrounding a dense central mass.

Give Titan huge articulated hands with thick, individually modelled stone fingers, distinct knuckles and a substantial thumb. The hands must open, curl and close into convincing fists. Use a few robust segments per finger so their construction remains clear during movement.

Torso, back and legs

Form the chest from wide, overlapping slabs that create a natural breastplate. Beneath it, smaller angled plates step inward toward the waist. Preserve deep shadowed crevices between these layers.

On the back, continue the same construction with a raised central ridge of stones and broad shoulder-blade plates. The rear silhouette should feel as finished as the front.

Use thick thighs, large angular knees, short powerful shins and broad feet with several chunky stone toes. The feet should spread sufficiently to support its enormous upper body.

Stone materials and moss

Use weathered grey stone with charcoal recesses, muted brown undertones and lighter worn edges. Give the rocks broad shaded faces, chipped bevels, shallow scratches, pits and occasional fractures.

Large silhouette changes and plate thickness must exist in the geometry. Fine cracks and surface grain can use textures or supported procedural shading.

Place irregular patches of vivid green moss across the shoulder tops, upper back, forearms and sheltered crevices, following the reference. Mix flat moss coverage with a few small raised clumps. Keep its distribution uneven so Titan feels ancient and naturally weathered.

The stone should have a mostly matte finish, with subtle variation between smoother exposed faces and rough broken surfaces. Maintain strong contact shadows between overlapping plates.

Keep the white-green eyes and green moss as its visual identity. During attacks, brief golden Earth energy can illuminate selected cracks and joints, then recede.

Summoning animation

A large amber Earth sigil spreads beneath the chosen summon position. Pebbles shake, dust lifts and cracks branch across the ground.

Titan rises through the surface in a deliberate sequence: shoulders and head emerge first, one enormous hand plants against the floor, then its torso and legs lift into view. It pushes itself upright, shedding dust and loose chips from its shoulders.

Its eyes ignite as it reaches its fighting stance. Finish the entrance with a heavy planted step and a low ring of dust.

Start the 25-second combat timer when Titan becomes active, after its entrance animation.

Movement and weight

Give Titan a slow, purposeful gait. Its torso shifts over the planted foot before the opposite leg advances. Every footfall produces a small dust puff, a few displaced pebbles and a subtle settling motion through the body.

Its arms swing with delayed momentum. Shoulder plates move with the shoulders, while smaller loose stones settle slightly afterward.

Animate the limbs through their segmented joints, preserving the rigidity of individual rock plates. Avoid visibly stretching solid stone like rubber.

Combat animations

Titan independently follows the party, approaches valid enemies and fights using three clear attack motions:

* Heavy punch: pull one shoulder backward, twist the torso, then drive the enormous fist forward. Release stone chips and a compact amber impact flash on contact.
* Sweeping backhand: rotate through the hips and shoulders, swinging one arm through a broad arc to strike nearby enemies. The fist leaves a short trail of dust and golden sparks.
* Ground slam: raise both fists, hold briefly to establish their weight, then smash them into the floor. Send an expanding Earth shockwave through nearby enemies, accompanied by temporary glowing cracks and lifted fragments.

Give each attack a readable windup, decisive contact and weighted recovery. Ground the damage timing in the actual punch, sweep or landing animation.

All attacks deal Earth damage. Use the existing summon and combat systems for damage scaling, health, targeting and navigation.
