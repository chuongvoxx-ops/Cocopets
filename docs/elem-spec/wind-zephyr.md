# Wind ultimate: Zephyr's Blessing — the user's spec (verbatim, 2026-11-07)

Row: key `zephyr` replaces Wind `skypalace` (Palace of the Sky), cost 100, cd 90, glyph 'leaf'.

Zephyr's Blessing — a wind ultimate that makes the entire party faster, harder to hit, and resistant to interruption.

The pet leaps upward and unfurls translucent butterfly wings made of emerald wind. One sweeping wingbeat sends a gentle
shockwave through the party, wrapping each ally in flowing wind ribbons. Small feather-shaped wisps orbit them, and every
movement leaves a soft mint-green afterimage.

For the duration, all allied pets gain:
* +30% movement speed.
* +20% attack speed.
* 20% damage reduction.
* Immunity to slows, with existing slows removed on activation.
* One Windguard charge: the next incoming hit is completely blocked, bursting into a spiral of feathers. The blocked hit
  also cannot stagger or knock back the ally.

The blessing travels with each pet, allowing player to switch between pets freely.

| Ultimate level | Duration | Additional benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Base | 10 seconds | All effects above |
| Level 4 | 13 seconds | Attack speed increases to +30% |
| Level 7 | 16 seconds | Attack speed increases to +35%; gain a second Windguard charge |

Suggested cooldown: 90 seconds, starting on activation. Recasting refreshes the blessing rather than stacking its bonuses.

Its signature visual: when a pet blocks a hit, its wind ribbons briefly unfold into enormous butterfly wings, shielding
it before dissolving back into flowing air.
