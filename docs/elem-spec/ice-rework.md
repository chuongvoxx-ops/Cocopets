The visual direction throughout is translucent blue ice, brilliant cyan edges, glowing internal fractures, refracted light, swirling snow, and glittering crystal fragments. The large shapes should remain visible inside the glow.

1. Diamond Dust
A thin spiral of frost travels across the ground before curved crystal petals rise around its centre. They curl inward and begin rotating, gathering smaller diamonds into a dense inner storm. The result resembles a magnificent ice flower spinning inside a travelling cyclone.
The outer petals rotate slowly enough to remain recognisable, while smaller crystals whip around the centre. Individual petals tilt, open, and close as the vortex moves. Cyan light runs along their edges, with lavender reflections flashing across their turning surfaces.
Level 1: One vortex with six large curved petals, a tightly spinning centre, and a flowing skirt of freezing mist.
Level 4: Two larger vortices. Each gains an inner petal layer rotating against the outer layer. They follow coordinated curved paths, briefly exchanging ribbons of diamond dust.
Level 7 Celestial: Three enormous flower vortices swirl across the battlefield simultaneously. Each contains three layered petal structures surrounding a luminous ice spindle. Their paths sweep around the enemy formation, leaving beautiful frosted trails. At the finish, each vortex opens into its fullest flower shape before releasing its petals in a spiralling shower of diamonds.

2. Frozen Swords
Narrow blue lines appear above the battlefield. Ice grows outward along them, forming colossal swords with translucent blades, deep-blue interiors, and brilliant central channels. Fine fragments orbit the swords as their edges finish crystallising.
They hang briefly with their tips pointed downward, then accelerate into the ground in a deliberate sequence. Each blade remains embedded while glowing fractures race outward beneath it. Jagged waves of ice rise along those fractures, expanding across the floor before breaking into glittering fragments.
Level 1: Two giant swords fall one after the other, each releasing a crystal wave and a powerful burst of blue light.
Level 4: Three larger swords strike in a triangular formation. Their glowing ground fractures connect, and their crystal waves spread in overlapping rhythms.
Level 7 Celestial: Five swords. Four plunge into the outer target area first. The fifth, significantly larger than the others, remains suspended above the centre while their light flows toward its landing point. It then impales the ground, releasing the widest and strongest icy wave. Its enormous blade cracks from tip to hilt before collapsing into descending blue shards.

3. Let It Go
A brilliant blue point expands into a giant hovering orb. Inside it, light circulates like currents beneath frozen water. Curved crystal plates rotate around its white-blue core, creating visible depth within the glow.
The surrounding ground freezes immediately when the orb activates. Frost spreads outward through branching veins, followed by a glassy frozen surface and a low fringe of icy mist.
Blue rays repeatedly shoot from the orb toward enemies. Each begins as a bright point travelling across its surface, gathers intensity, then extends into an icy lance. Small crystals briefly grow around struck enemies before shattering.
Level 1: Lasts 5 seconds. One rotating shell, a compact frozen field, and staggered individual rays.
Level 4: Lasts 8 seconds. Two crystal shells rotate in opposite directions. Rays fire in staggered pairs, and intricate frost patterns cover a wider area.
Level 7 Celestial: Lasts 12 seconds. A monumental orb with three transparent rotating shells floats above a broad frozen field. Multiple rays fire in coordinated sequences, drawing brilliant blue paths across the arena. At expiry, the shell plates separate, exposing the glowing core before the entire orb breaks into slowly turning fragments and falling snow.

4. Absolute Zero
A giant horizontal crystal forms above the pet, surrounded by rotating rings of ice. Particles pull toward its pointed tip as its internal light intensifies. It releases a massive frost beam with spiralling ribbons, tumbling shards, and freezing mist.
Level 1: One crystal and a focused beam.
Level 4: Three rotating rings shape a wider beam with two spiralling frost ribbons.
Level 7 Celestial: Several elongated crystal segments assemble into one enormous floating lance. Five rings rotate around it, aligning sequentially before releasing a colossal beam wrapped in three intertwining streams of ice.

5. Glacial Onslaught
Huge pointed ice crystals hover behind the pet, tilt toward enemies, then launch in staggered waves. Each crystal spins around its long axis, trailing blue light and snow. On impact, cracks illuminate its interior before it bursts into a forward-facing fan of shards.
Level 1: Three large crystal projectiles.
Level 4: Five crystals launch in a spreading formation.
Level 7 Celestial: Nine massive crystals assemble into three hovering rows. They fire in accelerating volleys, leaving braided trails across the battlefield. Each impact produces an expanding ring of frost and glittering fragments.

6. Frozen Singularity
A brilliant blue core appears between two enormous rotating ice rings. Loose shards spiral inward and accumulate around it, forming a suspended crystalline sphere. The rings tighten, the sphere fractures from within, and it detonates into a broad wave of ice.
Level 1: Two rings compress a small crystal core.
Level 4: Three tilted rings surround a larger core with multiple translucent layers.
Level 7 Celestial: Five intersecting rings rotate around a huge faceted sphere. They align into one plane as the core reaches maximum brightness. The sphere bursts outward, releasing a sweeping crystal shockwave while the rings expand and dissolve into diamond dust.
