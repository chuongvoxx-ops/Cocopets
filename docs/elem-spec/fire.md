# FIRE (keys in ELEM_MOVES.fire, in this order)
Vocabulary: curling flame sheets, ember streams, rolling ignition, molten shapes, heat shimmer and explosive release. Colours: deep red-orange bases (#C2410C, #FF7A3D), bright cores (#FFE08A, #FFF4C0), dark crust/char (#3A1A14) on molten shapes, blue-white at the hottest points. Flames are layered: dark outer tongue, orange body, yellow-white inner, additive glow. Burning ground: H.fire(x, y, r, life, dps) (the existing damage-over-time patch); burn status via dmgO({ burn: 1 }).

## STRONG
emberkoi — F-S01 Ember Koi: Two fish-shaped flame forms circle the pet, then swim through the air toward the target. Their ribbon tails leave curved ember trails. They cross on impact, creating a compact spiral burst with a clear two-hit rhythm.
cinderfan — F-S02 Cinder Fan: The pet sweeps a broad fan of flame forward. Its surface separates into distinct curling tongues, each shedding sparks from its tip. The fan strikes a close cone and leaves a brief semicircle of cooling embers.
furnaceseed — F-S03 Furnace Seed: A dense glowing coal lands at the selected position and cracks open as heat builds. Thin flames curl from its seams before it erupts upward. The delayed blast leaves a small burning patch using the existing damage-over-time system (H.fire).
moltenlash — F-S04 Molten Lash: A flexible ribbon of molten fire whips across the aimed arc. Its bright core stretches while darker crust-like fragments break from the outer surface. The tip snaps at the end of the sweep, creating a stronger small impact.
phoenixtalon — F-S05 Phoenix Talon: Three flaming claw marks descend diagonally across the selected area. Each mark persists briefly as a thin glowing groove before flaring. Feather-like embers rise from the crossing points, highlighting where the overlapping hits occur.
firewheel — F-S06 Firewheel Skip: A compact spinning wheel of flame bounces along the ground, leaving circular scorch patterns at each contact. Its outer ring flickers into short blade shapes. The last bounce breaks it into rolling embers that quickly cool.
candlewick — F-S07 Candlewick Chain: A thin flame trail races between nearby enemies in sequence. Each contact creates a small wick-like flare before the flame jumps onward. The connecting trails burn away from their starting points, making the chain's direction visually clear.
salamander — F-S08 Salamander Rush: A low salamander-shaped flame darts along the aimed route, weaving slightly while maintaining a predictable damage corridor. Its feet scatter bright sparks. At the endpoint, it coils into a compact explosion and leaves a curling tail of smoke.
hearthburst — F-S09 Hearthburst: Flames gather closely around the pet's feet, then roll outward as a low circular wave. The wave's leading edge is bright and clearly defined, while its interior quickly dims. Useful for clearing enemies surrounding the pet.
matchstars — F-S10 Falling Matchstars: A short fan of glowing sparks launches upward and falls into three nearby marked spots. Each spark stretches into a tiny comet before bursting. The staggered impacts create a compact area barrage with clear landing indicators (H.warn).

## ULTIMATES
armageddon — F-U01 Armageddon (replaces Phoenix of the Turning Sky, at the user's request): one huge tornado of flame with beautiful, properly shaded 3D fire. Each evolution stage adds damage. At Celestial (stage 7) it is renamed "Super Armageddon": four big fire tornadoes burn across the battlefield, leaving scorched earth and devastation.
embers — F-U02 Festival of a Thousand Embers: Floating fire lanterns emerge across the target area and rotate in rising tiers. They open sequentially, releasing carefully grouped streams of sparks onto marked impact zones. The remaining lanterns gather into a luminous canopy and burst together, producing a final shower of slow-falling ember ribbons.
crucible — F-U03 Solar Crucible: A bowl-shaped ring of molten plates forms above the selected zone, containing a swirling fire core. Flames pour through gaps as narrow damaging streams. The plates rotate open and release the core downward, creating a broad blast whose rolling flame edge cools into drifting black-and-gold flakes.
dragonkiln — F-U04 Dragonkiln Procession: Several furnace-like rings appear along the aimed route. A long flame dragon threads through them, gaining larger horns, fins, and a flowing mane at each passage. It strikes the route in successive surges before coiling around the final ring and detonating into a spiralling fire crown.
crimsonlotus — F-U05 Crimson Lotus Forge: Heavy molten petals rise around the selected area, their dark outer surfaces split by glowing seams. They unfold into a monumental fire lotus, releasing flame sweeps between layers. The central bud opens last and erupts vertically, while the petals fragment into cooling embers that arc away from the centre.
infernocarousel — F-U06 Inferno Carousel: Curved flame blades orbit a central column at several heights, forming a rotating carousel. Lower blades strike marked ground paths while upper blades provide depth and spectacle. The column compresses, launching the blades outward in successive rings, then releases a final low fire wave.
fallingsuns — F-U07 Crown of Falling Suns: Small blazing spheres assemble into a broad crown above the battlefield. Each sphere develops a rotating mantle of flame before falling into a marked location. After the staggered impacts, the crown's remaining outline descends as a final circular strike, shedding streams of bright cinders.
moltenriver — F-U08 River of Molten Stars: A ribbon of molten light flows forward across the ground, branching around its central path. Star-like fire cores travel inside it and erupt at intervals. At the endpoint, the stream curls upward into an enormous wave and folds back down, leaving briefly glowing channels that fade safely.
ninetails — F-U09 Nine-Tailed Conflagration: Nine long flame tails unfurl behind the pet in staggered arcs. Each tail lashes a different section of the forward combat area, leaving distinct curved ember paths. The tails braid into one massive spiral lance for the finishing strike, then separate into delicate, floating strands of fire.
paperflames — F-U10 World of Paper Flames: Thin flame sheets unfold into floating geometric banners around the target zone. Their edges burn progressively while the banners turn and sweep across enemies. The remaining sheets fold into a brilliant central shape, then open explosively into hundreds (dozens, thinned by H.n) of slow, curling ember fragments.
