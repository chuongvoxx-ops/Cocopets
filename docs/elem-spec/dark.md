# DARK (keys in ELEM_MOVES.dark, in this order)
Vocabulary: folding shadows, eclipses, ink-like tendrils, void structures, inward motion, distortion and collapse. Dark must stay visible on dark floors: every shape has violet (#B983F5 / #E0D2FF) edge light, pale silver-violet highlights and a glossy ink sheen; use deep blue-black bodies (#120B1E, #2A1F3A), never pure black fills without a lit rim. C.col #9C88D6, C.core #EDE6FF.

## STRONG
inkfang — D-S01 Inkfang: A dark droplet stretches into a curved fang and lunges forward close to the ground. Violet edge highlights keep its silhouette visible. On contact, it splashes into thin shadow ribbons that retract toward the impact point.
eclipsedisc — D-S02 Eclipse Disc: A black disc with a pale violet rim slides along the aimed direction. Its centre contains slowly rotating shadow patterns. It passes through enemies before turning edge-on and collapsing into a narrow vertical streak.
hem — D-S03 Shadow Hem: A dark ribbon draws a curved line across the ground, then rises into a low cutting curtain. Enemies crossing or standing on the line receive the timed strike. Loose ink-like fragments lift away from the curtain's top edge.
hollowlantern — D-S04 Hollow Lantern: A dark lantern forms over the selected area and opens to reveal a tiny void. Nearby shadow particles lean toward it before it releases a short damaging pulse (a light H.pull on monsters in it). The lantern closes sharply and falls apart into dim purple embers.
nightneedle — D-S05 Nightneedle Cluster: Several thin shadow needles gather around the pet at uneven heights. They launch in a staggered spread and briefly pin luminous dark marks to impacted enemies. The marks crumble into short downward trails after the hit feedback resolves.
undertow — D-S06 Velvet Undertow: A shallow pool of flowing darkness travels forward as a low wave. Its edges curl like folded fabric, pulling susceptible enemies slightly along its route (H.pull). The wave sinks into the floor, leaving small violet ripples.
ravenfold — D-S07 Ravenfold: Three abstract raven silhouettes unfold from the pet's shadow and dart toward the target. Each collapses into angular ribbons on impact. The final raven passes through the target and leaves a short fading trail shaped like an open wing.
umbralhook — D-S08 Umbral Hook: A crescent-shaped shadow hook shoots toward the target on a thin ink tether. It damages on contact and briefly pulls susceptible enemies toward the pet (H.pull over a few frames; bosses stay). The tether recoils into a tight spiral and disappears beneath the pet.
blackglass — D-S09 Blackglass Fracture: A reflective dark pane appears at the aimed point, its surface showing moving violet cracks. It snaps outward into a cone of shards. The shards flatten into shadow stains on landing, then evaporate.
double — D-S10 Midnight Double: A simplified shadow echo copies the pet's attack pose beside it (draw a dark silhouette with H.X's pet drawing is not available to you; draw an abstract pet-shaped blob with violet rim at the pet's size instead). The real pet releases a short forward strike, followed by the echo repeating a slightly wider version. The echo unravels from head to tail into curling dark threads.

## ULTIMATES
hollowmoon — D-U01 Eclipse of the Hollow Moon: A huge dark sphere forms above the target area, surrounded by a rotating silver-violet corona. Shadow streams rise toward it as repeated ground pulses damage enemies below. The sphere then compresses into a thin disc and collapses, sending a final expanding ring across the battlefield.
inkpalace — D-U02 Palace of Ink: Tall liquid-shadow panels rise and fold into the silhouette of a roofless palace around the selected zone. Tendrils flow down its walls and sweep across the ground. The structure folds inward panel by panel, finishing in a central splash of luminous-edged darkness that settles into fading ripples.
ravenfuneral — D-U03 Thousand-Raven Funeral: A broad shadow spreads beneath the target area and releases spiralling flocks of angular raven forms. The flocks dive through marked lanes before regrouping overhead. They merge into an immense winged silhouette that sweeps down once, bursting into scattered feathers with violet-lit edges.
abyssorrery — D-U04 Abyssal Orrery: Several black rings orbit an empty centre, their surfaces marked by slowly moving dim symbols. Small void nodes travel around the rings and fire converging shadow lances. The orbits tighten until the nodes collide, producing a controlled implosion (H.pull toward the centre) and an outward ripple of distorted space.
lastnight — D-U05 Curtain of the Last Night: Long dark ribbons descend around the target area like theatre curtains. They twist into moving vertical blades, sweeping inward in staggered pairs. The final pair parts to reveal a brief, starless void that releases a broad forward pulse, then closes into a thin fading seam.
forgottenkings — D-U06 Crown of Forgotten Kings: Broken crown segments rise from the floor and orbit a central shadow pillar. Each segment projects a long, clearly marked cutting shadow across the ground (use H.warnLine so the lanes read). The shadows rotate through a timed sequence before the crown assembles and drops, producing a final circular impact of dark glass and violet sparks.
leviathan — D-U07 Leviathan Beneath the Floor: An enormous serpentine shadow moves visibly beneath the target zone, its path outlined by dim luminous scales (drawUnder). Arched sections of its body breach the floor as damaging waves. Its head finally rises in a stylised silhouette, sweeps forward, and dissolves into a long downward cascade of ink.
blackrose — D-U08 Black Rose Compass: Four enormous thorn-like shadow needles form around the target area, connected by rotating dark arcs. They point inward, draw back, and strike in alternating pairs. A central geometric rose of folded shadow opens for the final burst, then loses its petals as thin blackglass fragments.
noreflection — D-U09 Night Without Reflection: Floating dark mirrors appear around enemies, each holding a simplified shadow echo. The echoes step partly from their surfaces and perform synchronised strikes. All mirrors then turn toward the centre and fracture inward, creating a dense final burst framed by clearly visible violet shard trails.
eventide — D-U10 Eventide Spiral: A towering corkscrew of darkness forms at the aimed point, wrapped in luminous violet filaments. Its lower coils sweep nearby enemies while particles flow upward through the centre. The spiral unwinds from top to bottom, converting each coil into an expanding ground wave before the final core collapses.
