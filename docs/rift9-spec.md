Dungeon 09 The Basilica of the Veiled Eye

Recommended level: 85. Affinity: Earth and Dark. Mythos connection: an original petrifying manifestation associated with Ghatanothoa. Seal Sigil: Veil.

Environment and route

A mountain monastery has fused with a sightless stone organism. Use volcanic black #242428, dusty ochre #A18E65, pale calcite #D8D1BB and a dangerous muted red #BF675D. Tall narrow pillars surround broad open walkways. Cloth banners are replaced by rigid stone sheets that hang without bending. Every decorative statue is an abstract beast curled around an eye-shaped stone, avoiding a crowded field of humanoid figures.

The entry stair rises between two monolithic closed eyelids carved into the mountain. Inside, red light falls from narrow ceiling slits, creating visible stripes across the floor. Keep noninteractive light stripes desaturated and unoutlined so they cannot be confused with attack lanes. Large walls lean inward slightly, giving the architecture a watchful posture.

Route: the Blind Pilgrimage through the outer cloister; a small ossuary for boss one; the Witness Containment courtyard; a circular reliquary for boss two; the Procession of Stone; and a final 38-metre square sanctuary. Its northern wall is occupied by a massive sealed eye, with the final boss crouched below it. Four movable stone screens sit on short rails in the arena.

Three distinct dungeon challenges

The basilica contains a stationary-cover navigation puzzle, a monster-containment combat ritual and a moving-cover procession. Each section includes its own monster packs and a separate exit requirement.

Monster roster

• Calcite leeches: pale smooth cones with a circular black mouth and three red eyes down the spine. They spit slow, stack-building petrification droplets.
• Witness scarabs: flat black beetles carrying a large upright red eye plate on their backs. They project a narrow, rotating cone and turn slowly enough to flank.
• Broken sentinels: four-legged stone beasts with rectangular torsos and missing heads. A small red orb floats where the head should be. They charge only after the orb locks a straight line to the player.

Dungeon section 1 The Blind Pilgrimage

Route and appearance: ascend between two mountain-sized closed eyelids into the outer cloister. Three red eye pylons overlook broad stone paths. Pale rectangular screens stand on short black rails. Their shadows form double-bordered safe regions with closed-eye icons. Rigid stone banners hang above the route.

Unique challenge — move cover to cross watched ground: slide each screen between its two marked stops to block the next pylon’s visible cone, then cross through the protected shadow. Pylons turn among three fixed angles with a preview before each turn. Being seen builds three Calcification stages; leaving the cone removes them. Reaching three causes a brief root and damage, followed by automatic release.

Monsters: two calcite leeches and two broken sentinels defend the first screen. A witness scarab and two leeches guard the final crossing. Defeat the scarab or approach around its slow cone before moving the cover.

Completion and recovery: reach the last screen’s shadow and clear its guards. Screen positions remain reversible. The final safe zone contains a latch opening Eshra’s ossuary; turning the camera away is never the puzzle solution.

Boss 1 Eshra the Many-Lidded

Appearance: a four-metre calcite scorpion with a low rectangular body and six short jointed legs. Instead of a stinger, its tail ends in a large red eye framed by three stone lids. Its claws are broad shields with black inner surfaces. The tail slowly surveys the arena independently of the body, then stops decisively before firing.

Arena: a 28-metre ossuary with three permanent square pillars spaced in a wide triangle.

Mechanics: Witness Ray marks the handler with a red tether and takes three seconds to fire. Break the tether by moving behind a pillar. Claw Crush threatens a close frontal wedge, encouraging flanking while the eye tracks separately. At 50%, the eye marks one pillar to crack after the next shot. Choose another pillar for the following ray; leave the cracked pillar intact for one extra cycle so the transition is readable.

Dungeon section 2 The Witness Containment

Route and appearance: Eshra’s ossuary opens into a broad calcite courtyard containing three circular purification basins. Each basin has a high pale rim interrupted by two walkable gaps, with a closed-eye symbol in its centre. Above the exit, three black stone locks resemble sealed eyelids.

Unique challenge — defeat witnesses in the containment zones: lure one eye-bearing witness scarab into each basin and defeat it there. The contained eye energy closes one exit lock. Use pet Hold or Recall to control where the kill occurs, while avoiding the scarab’s turning cone. This is a combat-positioning objective rather than another cover maze.

Monsters: three scarabs enter one at a time from marked pens. Each is accompanied by two leeches; a broken sentinel supports the last pen. Keep each active group small enough to read its sightlines and lure path.

Completion and recovery: purify all three basins and defeat the supporting enemies. A scarab killed outside a basin drops a recoverable eye stone that can be carried into an empty basin, so pet damage cannot permanently break the objective. The three locks retract and reveal Thessarym’s reliquary.

Boss 2 Thessarym the Veil Serpent

Appearance: an eight-metre snake formed from seven pale stone segments joined by dark flexible gaps. Each segment contains one small closed eye. Its head is broad and triangular, with two hooked side fins and no visible jaw. A red vertical eye opens at the front only while attacking. Its body coils into a low ring, allowing the player to see and move across the arena without the model obscuring everything.

Arena: a 32-metre circular reliquary with two sliding screens on north-south rails and a central safe movement lane.

Mechanics: Passing Judgement opens body eyes in a numbered order; corresponding narrow gaze lanes fire sequentially. Move through a lane after its eye closes. Unveiling locks a broad frontal beam onto the player, then freezes its direction; pull a screen into the beam and move behind it. At 50%, the serpent starts a tail sweep while the beam is charging. Place the screen early, then use the safe side of it to avoid both. Screen travel takes one second between fixed stops.

Dungeon section 3 The Procession of Stone

Route and appearance: Thessarym’s reliquary exits onto a straight ritual avenue with two side-latch alcoves. A broad pale screen travels along a central rail. Three high eye pylons watch successive sections of the avenue. The sanctuary’s massive closed eye is visible beyond the final arch.

Unique challenge — advance inside moving shelter: follow the screen’s safe shadow as it moves between three stops. At the first two, command the pet to hold the screen’s handle while the handler enters the protected side alcove and opens a latch. Once the latch is open, release the handle to continue. The screen never leaves while an essential latch remains closed.

Monsters: two leeches block the first stop; a sentinel and two leeches block the second; a witness scarab defends the final arch. Fight from shelter or wait for the pylon’s announced turn before flanking.

Completion and recovery: open both latches, reach the end of the rail and defeat the scarab. A failed gaze returns the handler to the latest stop with damage. The screen parks as part of the sanctuary wall, opening the way to Gharaal.

Boss 3 Gharaal the Buried Witness

Appearance: a colossal squat creature resembling a living basalt altar. Its central body is a ten-metre-wide faceted mound supported by eight low, thick limbs. Five calcite horns rise unevenly along its back, like ruined columns. Its face is one enormous red eye sealed behind four wedge-shaped stone lids. Two small arms beneath the eye end in flat paddles used to drag screens. Cracks along the body glow dull red when the eye is about to open. It moves by heaving its whole mass, not walking like a humanoid.

Mechanics: above 70%, Slabfall marks three rectangular impacts and exposes one limb for a counterattack. From 70–35%, The Unveiling opens the central eye for a major gaze. Position a screen so its shadow covers the handler and pet before the cast completes. Gharaal then drags that screen one rail stop, forcing a deliberate reposition. Below 35%, Witnesses Within opens two smaller back eyes. Their cones leave a visible intersection of safe shadows. Slide one screen, stand in the shared safe zone, then destroy a calcite eye node to force the main eye closed. The second cone activates two seconds after the first so players can read the solution. No unavoidable damage is dealt merely because the camera happens to face the boss.

Victory: the eye in the wall closes permanently, calcified plants regain colour and the stone banners crumble into harmless dust. Reward a closed-eye talisman and the Veil Sigil.
